Data source

PriceCatcher

Prices are collected and verified daily by groundstaff under Malaysia’s Ministry of Domestic Trade. Codes are mapped through official lookup tables.

View official catalogue ↗

Storage design

Built for free tiers

Raw detail is kept only for the latest 30 days. Older months become compact summaries so the project stays within free database limits.

Incremental · idempotent · reproducible

AI boundary

Explain, don’t calculate

All price statistics are calculated by code. AI may explain validated movements, but it will not calculate the underlying metrics.

Deterministic by design
Source: Ministry of Domestic Trade · PriceCatcher For price surveillance and exploration, not a replacement for CPI.
